# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students, independent learners, and study groups using laptops, tablets, phones and fullscreen focus sessions.

## Product Purpose

Sanctra combines focus timers, planning, saved study history, analytics, group conversations, rankings, audio and a bounded planning assistant.

## Positioning

A personal, atmospheric study workspace with its own identity. References inform specific usability improvements; they do not dictate a replacement visual identity.

## Operating Context

Long sessions need calm scenery, readable controls, stable timers and useful local time. Group rooms prioritize conversation and study standings together on desktop, with accessible tabs on smaller screens.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve count-up, countdown, Pomodoro, persistence, session records and keyboard behavior.
- Preserve account isolation, Firebase synchronization, local caching, subscriptions and existing access rules.
- JEE Track is an optional preparation workspace. Accounts opt in through the visible Explore JEE Track sidebar entry or Settings → Preferences; its preparation tools stay inactive until enabled. It starts with empty personal records and a sourced preparation chapter map, uses private per-record Firestore sync, shared Sanctra tasks and tagged saved timer sessions, and calls the existing Sanctra AI gateway. AI can propose chapter-linked shared tasks and past sessions; a completed timer session can receive an editable chapter suggestion from its description. Admin can pause it independently without deleting records. The owner enabled the operational feature. Preserve the independent admin pause control. New accounts start opted out; existing account choices are retained.
- AI suggestions remain bounded to Sanctra planning and recorded activity, with reviewable changes and usage limits.
- Appearance work must preserve routes, data and real behavior unless a functional change is explicitly authorized.
- Deploy each authorized stage only after build, relevant behavior checks and actual browser inspection.

## Brand Commitments

- Calm, personal, modern and lightly playful; never visually dull or overloaded.
- Preserve the newly added backgrounds and user-selected scenery, including across navigation and reloads.
- Neutral glass surfaces with local contrast, rather than a heavy blanket dimming the background.
- Tasteful sky blue, blush and soft gold accents. Avoid muddy purple, sage-green washes, brown glass and neon ornament.
- Landing colors follow the approved illustrated meadow video, separate from the timer background library.
- Use genuine imagery, real capabilities and explicitly labeled demonstrations. Never invent users, statistics or testimonials.
- Keep existing free/Plus entitlements; a visual refresh does not change pricing or access.

## Evidence on Hand

The current implementation, supplied screenshots and the owner's latest decisions. Older redesign proposals are superseded and are not design authority.

## Product Principles

1. Make the timer, current task and message field immediately usable.
2. Spend screen space on work, conversation and records rather than large headers.
3. Use one authoritative layout for each component; remove superseded rules.
4. Keep management actions discoverable without crowding normal reading.
5. Verify the rendered product, not just successful compilation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast over scenery, visible keyboard focus, labeled controls and usable touch targets. Respect reduced motion and keep time displays stable with tabular numerals.
