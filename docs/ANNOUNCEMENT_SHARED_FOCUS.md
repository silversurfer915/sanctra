# Shared progress, a calmer soundtrack

## One global study community
Registered accounts now appear automatically in the global study rankings. Compare saved study time for today, this week and this month. Existing accounts have been added too.
- Rankings use study time alone; Plus+ never changes your score.
- Zero time shows **—**. Equal totals share a rank.
- Plus+ profiles carry their custom banner, avatar styling and membership tag into global and group rankings. Trial memberships show a **Trial** tag.

## Your target, connected
Choose your target examination from the profile dropdown and set your exam session date. Sanctra creates the linked primary Exam Goal and countdown. Change the goal’s date when your official schedule is confirmed.

## Focus audio, made simpler
Explore ten offline ambient sounds in a calmer card layout. Search on YouTube, paste a sound link and save your favourites to your account.
- Close the panel and keep listening.
- A compact ambient control stays available; YouTube keeps a visible player with its own playback controls.
- Your saved library follows you across devices.

## Better support and administration
The team can activate memberships and trials for exact durations, handle support tickets and inspect account progress to resolve issues. Administrator access is controlled; admins cannot promote themselves. Guest sessions are separated from registered accounts in the admin directory.
