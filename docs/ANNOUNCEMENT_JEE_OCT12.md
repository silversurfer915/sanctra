# 🎓 Meet JEE Track — your preparation, connected

Your study desk now has an optional home for JEE preparation. ✨

**📚 Keep your preparation together**
Track Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics chapters, record Mains and Advanced mocks, log practice questions, and organise backlogs. Your progress starts with your own records — no sample scores or made-up achievements.

**🗓️ One connected plan**
Chapter-linked tasks use Sanctra’s shared planner. Scheduled mocks and dated backlogs appear in your calendar, and tagged focus sessions contribute to JEE study hours.

**🤖 Plan and log with Sanctra AI**
Ask Sanctra AI to plan chapter practice or log a completed study session. Review the suggested details before saving. Prefer to study first? Describe what you studied when saving your timer session and ask AI to suggest the chapter — you can adjust it yourself.

**🌱 Choose what belongs in your workspace**
JEE Track is opt-in. Open **Explore JEE Track** in the sidebar and select **Enable JEE Track** to begin. Already enabled it? Open **JEE Track** directly.

Your usual focus timer, tasks and study history stay connected. Let’s make the next session count. 💙
