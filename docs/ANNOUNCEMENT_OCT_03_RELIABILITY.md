# A calmer, more reliable Sanctra

A collection of improvements to help you settle in, study together and see your progress clearly.

## Your study space
- Refined glass surfaces, theme colours and text readability across desktop and iPad layouts.
- Scenic backgrounds with still-image fallbacks and improved video delivery for Safari.
- A refreshed landing page with interactive previews and clearer descriptions of Sanctra’s actual features.
- A new Profile Studio with coordinated signature collections, animated avatars and previews of how your identity appears in the community.

## Studying together
- Groups open to the focus leaderboard, with conversations in their own tab.
- Your messages appear on the right and other members’ messages on the left. Type @ to mention someone, reply to messages and share images.
- Support conversations support image attachments, with clearer ticket tools and alerts for administrators when tickets need attention.
- Product updates can appear once, daily or on every refresh, with expiry and dismissal controls.

## Progress that stays consistent
- Global rankings include registered members, preserve earlier credited study time and deduct eligible ranked time when a saved study session is removed.
- Public ranking totals are now separate from private timer details. Device identifiers and timer diagnostics stay private.
- Ranking loading states recover after partial failures, and member/profile listeners avoid unnecessary repeated reads.
- Admin access and paid Plus+ membership are handled separately, so removing an admin role preserves an existing paid subscription. Role changes update badges together, and administrators cannot freely rewrite another member’s public profile.
- The profile’s primary exam and date come from Goals. Editing your profile does not create duplicate exam goals.
- Focusing a task syncs the task and timer setup while leaving you in control of when the timer starts.

## The latest repairs
- Fixed compressed text areas and misplaced labels in profile, announcement and support forms.
- Fixed stale group-inspection results, repeated timer-review lookups and invalid chat allowance counts.
- Added stricter code and test checks, stronger new invite codes and checks for profile image links.

Refresh Sanctra to load this update. If you spot something that still needs attention, send a screenshot through Help & Feedback.
