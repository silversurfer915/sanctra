# A clearer Sanctra, everywhere

## What changed

- Background motion now flows visibly across the landing page and abstract focus-room wallpapers, without zooming the artwork in and out.
- Glass panels are frostier and more opaque, so text stays readable over every theme and scenic wallpaper.
- Planner dates, mobile layouts, timer controls and account dialogs now stay balanced and centered across desktop, iPad and phone.
- Group chat uses each member’s current profile identity across devices. Group settings are easier to read on iPad.
- Members with no focused time now show **—** instead of receiving a leaderboard rank.
- Inbox announcement previews are clean and readable. New announcements show an unread badge, and major product updates open once in a dedicated **What’s new** view.
- Signing out now asks for confirmation.

> Thank you for the direct feedback. These fixes are live now, and the next releases will build out Sanctra Plus and focused audio one step at a time.
