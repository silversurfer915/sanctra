# ✨ A little more room to focus

Your Sanctra desk has had a refresh. More room to talk, a calmer Settings space, and little touches that make a long study day feel better. 💜

## 💬 Study together, stay in the conversation
- **Chat and rankings, side by side:** desktop and wide tablet group rooms now give chat its own generous space, with independently scrolling rankings and a composer that stays in view.
- **Clearer replies:** quoted messages have their own preview, so you can follow a conversation without decoding repeated text.
- **A little expression:** choose an emoji right from the composer. Attachments and message actions stay close at hand.
- **Catch up easily:** a new-message divider marks incoming messages while you are reading earlier messages or looking at another group tab.
- **Less clutter:** consecutive messages are grouped and the room header takes up less space.

## 🎨 A softer, more personal space
- **Refreshed Settings:** cooler glass, pastel accents and compact navigation replace the heavy grey layout.
- **A cleaner background library:** search scenes, filter the collection and manage your own media in one place.
- **Thoughtful colour:** lavender, mint and peach give the group room more personality while keeping your scenery visible.

## 🕒 Small details, better focus
- **Live local time** is available on the Focus Room, including timer-only and fullscreen views.
- Phone and iPad layouts have been refined across the timer, calendar, Settings and other workspaces, with clearer controls and better spacing.

> Your existing sessions, groups and personal backgrounds stay with you. Open a group or Settings to try the refresh. 🌿

Thanks for studying with Sanctra. Take your next chapter at your own pace.
