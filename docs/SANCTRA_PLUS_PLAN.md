# Sanctra Plus — pricing and launch scope

Owner pricing (2 October 2026): ₹59 monthly; ₹159 yearly; limited early-user yearly offer ₹150. These are planned prices, not a live subscription or checkout. The annual offer is unusually generous; keep service costs bounded instead of bundling costly unlimited services.

## Keep the free core useful

Timer, timer-only focus, session history, personal analytics, task scheduling, basic themes, existing wallpaper selections, meditation and ordinary support remain available. Do not retroactively lock users out of backgrounds or profile choices they already use. Exam rankings are opt-in and never improve with payment.

## A worthwhile personal upgrade

- Animated avatar and tasteful Plus tag, with reduced-motion support and static fallback.
- Curated animated profile scenes, profile accent choices and frame collections.
- Extra exclusive wallpaper packs and personal wallpaper collections. Existing free animation remains free.
- Custom group invitation codes and additional group customization.
- Higher chat allowance, such as 500/day versus a future 250/day free allowance; separate short-burst spam limits for both plans. Exact quotas require the later quota rollout; current release does not impose these proposed limits.
- Saved focus presets for work, learning, meditation and exam preparation.
- More export layouts, personalized weekly summaries and optional goal templates.
- Early access to new customization packs and beta features, with the same baseline reliability for everyone.
- Priority placement in the support queue, not a guaranteed human response time until staffing can support it.

Avoid unlimited AI/video storage or a personal designer bundled into ₹159/year. Bespoke interface/profile design is a separately quoted one-time request. Plus can include preset customization; custom commissioned work must have its own scope and price.

## Before accepting payments

Define the early-offer eligibility period, whether ₹150 applies to renewals, tax treatment, cancellation/refund policy, entitlement expiry and renewal behavior. Integrate payments and server-managed entitlements with purchase verification and idempotent webhook processing. Never grant Plus from a browser-written settings flag. Apply upload size/type limits and moderation to animated media. No active subscription, fake entitlement or payment form has been added in this release.

The yearly tier is about ₹13.25/month; treat this as a launch growth offer with monitored Firebase/storage/support costs. Keep future pricing changes explicit and preserve purchases already made on their agreed terms.
