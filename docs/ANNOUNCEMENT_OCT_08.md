# Today’s Sanctra updates · October 8

## Timer & rankings
- **Server-timed normal sessions:** time during laptop sleep or background use is credited when you reconnect. Strict mode still pauses when you leave the foreground. Existing session caps remain.
- Ranking sync now retries temporary connection failures and gives clearer error messages.
- Fixed group rankings counting the same live session twice, so verified group and global totals use the same recorded time.

## Group chat & inbox
- Faster message sending, with fewer steps before delivery.
- Group mentions now update the Inbox unread badge even while you’re on another page.
- **Cleaner message actions:** hover over a message on desktop, or tap its small arrow on mobile, for Reply, Copy and Remove. Removal permissions stay the same.

- Add your own background image from your device or a link, alongside custom video links.
- Updated iPad status-bar styling and profile spacing.

## Profiles & Plus+
- A cleaner profile editor, five new animated companion portraits, and personal GIF/MP4/WebM links for Plus+ members.

## Help & Sanctra AI
- Categorized support forms with FAQs, including **Plus+ purchase and subscription requests**. Follow ticket status and admin replies in your Inbox.
