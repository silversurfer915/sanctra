# A little more you. A little more together.

Sanctra is growing into a sanctuary for **learning, meaningful work and mindful time**. Here’s what’s new.

## Your space, your pace
- A redesigned profile with your focus intention, a short bio, status message, illustrated avatar choices and weekly target.
- Choose exam preparation, general learning, deep work or meditation when you first enter. Change your choice anytime in Profile.
- A cleaner, themed look for sign-in, saved-session and streak dialogs.

## Focus together, live
- Create public or invitation-only groups, join or leave, invite friends and talk in group chat.
- Owners can manage their group, appoint admins and delete it. Members, focus status and rankings update live.
- Daily, weekly and monthly group rankings, with movement compared with the previous period.
- Optional global focus rankings for competitive-exam preparation, with an all-exams view and a filter for your exam. Rankings use saved focus and shared UTC periods; they are **not a measure of exam readiness**.

> Competition is your choice. Work, learning and meditation do not join global rankings. Opting in shares only your chosen profile details and focus totals; your email and session notes stay private.

## A helping hand
- Support is now open: create a private ticket for bugs, account questions, suggestions, community concerns or collaborations.
- Read team replies and continue the conversation in the same ticket. Resolve or reopen it whenever needed.
- Announcements now support clearer headings, lists, links, callouts and illustrated covers.

## Recent improvements
- Detailed day, week and month analytics, session dates, start/end times, durations and notes.
- Scheduled tasks with subjects and a current-task bar that appears only on its scheduled day. “Focus on task” starts a countdown for the task’s time slot.
- A centered timer-only view that hides the surrounding interface.
- More coordinated illustrated backgrounds, flowing abstract animation and time-based guest themes: Blue Mist in the morning, Lavender in the afternoon and Charcoal at night.

## What’s next
Music and soundscapes, a YouTube player, an optional syllabus tracker, additional group and message quotas, richer profile customization and **Sanctra Plus** are planned.

Plus pricing is planned at **₹59/month or ₹159/year**, with a limited **₹150/year early-user offer**. Animated identity, exclusive wallpaper packs, custom group codes, higher chat allowances and priority support are being prepared. Paid plans and checkout are not live yet; existing free focus tools remain available. Bespoke interface requests will be quoted separately.

Private groups are invitation-only for members; the platform owner can review group messages and members for moderation. Support tickets remain visible only to their author and the Sanctra team.
