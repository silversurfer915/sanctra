# One home. More room for your focus.

## Meet your focus spaces
Keep exam preparation, learning, work and mindful time together in Sanctra, with a separate space for each.

- Create and switch spaces from the focus room. Each has its own subjects, projects or practices, tasks, deadlines, goals and history.
- Your timer and saved sessions stay connected to the space you focused in.
- Explore one space in Analytics, or include all your spaces for an overall view.
- Existing records remain in your original space. Archiving a space keeps its history safe.

## A better connection between your plans and focus
Link tasks to exam deadlines or work milestones. Set your own dates and goals. Exam rankings remain optional for exam preparation; work and mindfulness spaces keep their own pace.

## Clearer, calmer everywhere
We have improved text contrast, frosted surfaces, modal typography, settings, date controls and announcement previews. Phone layouts now give the timer priority, with clearer controls and less cramped progress cards. The reset confirmation follows your theme too.

We also corrected theme flashes at startup, subject selection after refreshing a new space, guest-space merging, and the what's-new popup. Read updates remain available in your Inbox.

## Still ahead
We will continue refining the experience before introducing Sanctra Plus. VIP plans and checkout are not part of this release. Thank you for making room for learning, meaningful work and a quieter moment.
