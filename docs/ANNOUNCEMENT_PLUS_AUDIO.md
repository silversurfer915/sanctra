# More ways to make Sanctra yours

## Sanctra Plus+ is available
Animated avatars, personal profile scenes, motion wallpapers, custom group codes, higher daily chat allowances and priority support are now available through manual membership activation.
- **₹59/month**, **₹159/year**, or the limited **₹99/year early-user offer**.
- Open a private support ticket to request payment details. A team member confirms payment and activates your membership. There are no automatic charges.

## Study together, listen your way
- Global study rankings now compare saved study time for today, this week and this month.
- Zero study time stays unranked. Equal totals share a position; Plus+ never boosts your score.
- Ten generated ambient sounds are ready offline, including rain, beach waves, flowing water, forest textures and quieter noise options.
- Save your own YouTube ambient links in Your sounds and play them in the visible player.

## A clearer focus room
The duplicate bottom Timer only control is removed; use the small control at the top. Timer progress handles breaks correctly, and announcement pages use a cleaner reading layout.

## Coming next
A lecture playlist will let you save video links ahead of time and switch between lessons inside Sanctra. An optional syllabus tracker is also coming. More profile and administration improvements are being prepared.
