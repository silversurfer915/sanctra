# Your desk, connected to you

## Sign in to settle in
Sanctra now requires a Google or email account to open a study desk. Guest entry buttons, guest sessions and simulated local logins have been removed. Anonymous access is also disabled in Firebase.

The landing page still has local examples so you can explore the timer, tasks and group conversation before signing in. Every button that opens a real workspace now leads to sign-in.

## A more considered first impression
- A smaller, glassy focus preview with clearer timer controls and a coordinated scenic background.
- A refreshed sign-in card that follows your selected landing scene, instead of changing colours with the time of day.
- Clearer email forms, keyboard navigation and account entry messages.

## Scenery, without the gradients
Only image and video backgrounds remain. Gradient wallpapers and their animation setting have been removed. Old gradient selections automatically move to the illustrated scene with the same colour palette, including Cloudside Study Desk for Blue Mist.

## Reliability repairs released today
- Fixed compressed text areas and misplaced form labels.
- Fixed ranking loading after partial failures and reduced repeated profile reads.
- Kept existing ranked study totals while separating public scores from private timer/device details.
- Made admin grants atomic and blocked standalone edits to other members’ public identity.
- Fixed stale group-inspection results, repeated timer-review lookups and invalid chat counts.
- Added strict app/test checks, safer profile image links and stronger new invite codes.

The earlier “A calmer, more reliable Sanctra” update in your inbox covers the wider improvements to profiles, groups, support, goals, audio and study spaces.
