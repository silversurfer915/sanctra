# A calmer Sanctra, with more ways to focus

Sanctra is fully free — a place for study, focused work, meditation, and a little room to breathe.

New and improved
• Day, week, and month analytics, past-date navigation, subject breakdowns, goal progress, and clearer focus trends.
• Detailed session history with start/end times, focused duration, elapsed time, full notes, date filters, and search.
• Tasks with dates, subjects, topics, and time slots. Your timer shows today’s current or next timed task; tomorrow’s schedule stays hidden today.
• “Focus on task” now starts the timer: use the remaining time during a slot, or the full planned duration when starting early. Unfinished focus is saved before switching tasks.
• Coordinated pastel themes, improved contrast and alignment, a branded loading screen, and a smoother abstract light animation that replaces zooming and panning.
• Three new illustrated backgrounds: Golden Reading Nook, Rose Garden Terrace, and Moonlit Library, alongside the existing favorites.
• Signed-in users return to the timer on refresh. Announcement management and moderation tools have also been improved.

Coming next
Relaxing music, a YouTube player, improved Groups, in-app support tickets and replies, and an optional syllabus tracker for those who want it, plus further refinements to the focus experience. These features are planned, not available yet; Groups and Support remain under construction while we improve them.

Your saved sessions, subjects, and tasks are preserved. Thank you for helping shape Sanctra.
