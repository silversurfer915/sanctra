A few
quiet answers.

Can I use Sanctra for free?

Yes. The core timer, tasks, exam goals, personal analytics and study groups are free. Plus+ is an optional, manually activated membership for additional personalization and higher group-chat limits. You can begin with the free tools and explore Plus+ through Support.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign in with Google or create a free email account to use your study desk, save sessions and join groups. The examples on this page let you explore the tools before signing in; example timers, tasks and messages are not saved to an account.

What do group rankings measure?

Daily, weekly and monthly rankings preserve historical study credit and add new verified online timer time. A server-clock check limits score increments and prevents concurrent devices from adding time. After the verification update, manual, imported and unverified offline sessions remain in personal analytics but do not add competitive credit. A timer cannot establish whether someone is actually studying.

What is included in Plus+ customization?

Plus+ adds coordinated profile collections, animated avatars, frames and auras, profile banners, and curated video backgrounds. You can preview an identity in your profile, ranking and conversation layouts before saving. Your chosen appearance travels with your public study identity.

Can I study alone?

Absolutely. Groups are optional. Use a timer, organize your subjects, set a daily goal and review your own progress. When you want company, join a public group or enter an invitation code for a private one.

Can I get help or report a problem?

Open a private Support ticket from your desk. Add details and images, follow replies in the ticket, and check notifications for updates. Study-group conversations also support image attachments, replies and @mentions.
